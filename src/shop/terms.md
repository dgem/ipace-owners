## Who can order

Anyone can order; membership is not required. The seller’s name and postal address are
shown in the shop before checkout. For all order enquiries, email
[contact@ipace-owners.org](mailto:contact@ipace-owners.org).

## Prices and payment

Prices and payments are in GBP. This shop is not intended to generate profit. Prices cover
production, delivery, supplier taxes and payment costs, with prices rounded up to end in
.49 or .99. Any surplus contributes to the website’s running costs, currently around £30
per month. This is a purchase of merchandise, not a charitable donation.

Product listings show starting production prices. Enter your delivery address to receive
the total delivered price, then check your basket and address before paying. Quotes are valid
for 30 minutes. The checkout total is the amount charged by Stripe. Your bank may charge
for converting from another currency. Your carrier may collect import duties or local
import charges separately.

## Making and delivering your order

Printify and its print providers make and ship the merchandise. Available destinations depend
on the selected products and shipping providers. Products may be made in different countries
and arrive in separate parcels. We aim to deliver within 30 days; if we cannot do so, we will
contact you to agree a revised date or arrange a refund. Printify sends shipping updates to
the email address you supply. Contact us if an order is late, damaged or missing.

## Cancellation and returns

Contact us as soon as possible if you need to change or cancel an order. Manufacturing may
already have started, but that does not remove your statutory rights. Standard group designs
are not treated as personalised solely because they are printed after ordering.

For standard merchandise, you may tell us you wish to cancel within 14 days of delivery,
then return the goods within the following 14 days. You pay the return postage for a change
of mind. We refund the purchase and standard outbound delivery within 14 days after receiving
the return or evidence that it has been sent. We may reduce a refund for handling beyond
what is necessary to inspect the goods. Contact us for return instructions before posting;
do not send returns to a print provider without instructions.

Faulty, damaged or incorrectly supplied goods are handled separately: contact us with your
order reference and details so we can arrange an appropriate remedy. Nothing in these terms
limits your statutory rights. Cancelling fulfilment at Printify and refunding your payment
are separate actions; contact us to confirm both have been handled.

You may use this cancellation message by email:

> I wish to cancel my merchandise order. Order reference: ____. Ordered on: ____.
> Received on: ____. Name: ____. Delivery address: ____. Date: ____.

## Your information

We use your contact and delivery details to fulfil your order and provide order support.
Stripe processes payment information; Printify and its providers receive the delivery
information needed to fulfil your order. Ordering does not enrol you as a member or add you
to marketing lists. See our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
