We are sorry that some members have experienced a sign-in loop or a page that did not finish
loading after using a magic link. Signing in should be the easy part of taking part, and we know
that this has been frustrating.

We have improved the sign-in recovery path and added a short **sign-in code** to any recoverable
error. The code lets us follow the technical decisions made during that journey without asking
you to share your passwordless link, account token, or other private information.

## If you see a sign-in error

Please send us:

- the sign-in code shown in the message (for example, `IP-ABCD-2345`);
- the approximate time it happened, including your time zone if you are outside the UK;
- the page or action you were trying to use;
- your device and browser, for example “iPhone, Safari” or “Windows, Chrome”; and
- a screenshot if it helps — but please make sure it does **not** show your magic link, email
  address, or any other personal information.

Please do **not** forward the email sign-in link itself. It is private and can be used to sign in.
Send the details to [contact@ipace-owners.org](mailto:contact@ipace-owners.org).

## If the site looks out of date

The site asks browsers to fetch a fresh page whenever it is reloaded. If you do not see the
sign-in code on an error, or the page clearly looks different from the current site, you may
still have an older page open.

1. Close any open I-PACE Owners site tabs and reload the page.
2. If that does not help, clear the website data for **ipace-owners.org only** in your browser's
   site-data or privacy settings, then reopen the site.
3. Clearing site data signs you out, so request a new magic link before trying again.

There is no need to clear all of your browser history, passwords, or cookies. If you are unsure
where to find the site-data setting, send us the device and browser details above and we will help.

Thank you for your patience, and for helping us make the site more reliable for everyone.
