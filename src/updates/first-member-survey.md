Our first member survey is now open. It asks which outcomes you would find acceptable for our
discussions with JLR ahead of the September meeting, with a particular focus on the HV battery,
long waits for parts, and wider reliability and safety concerns.

You can select every outcome you would support and, if you wish, mark one as your preferred
outcome. Some options also offer space for an optional short detail. Your experience will help us
understand where members' priorities align and where further work is needed.

## A fairer way to vote

We do not show live results before you have submitted your own response. That keeps the vote
independent. After you submit, you can review the aggregate results and update your response
while the survey remains open. No free-text responses are published to members.

## Take the survey

You need to be signed in as a member. If you have not signed in recently, use your member email
address to request a secure sign-in link.

[Take the member survey →](/member/surveys/)

Thank you for taking a few minutes to help steer the group's work with JLR.
