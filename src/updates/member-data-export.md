A member asked for a straightforward way to take a copy of everything they have recorded
with the group. That is now available from **Export your data** on the signed-in
[My Data account page](/member/account/).

## Two useful formats

- **Separate CSV files** — a ZIP containing individual files for membership details,
  vehicles, State of Health readings, and service or fault history. CSV is a widely
  supported, machine-readable format that is useful for moving or analysing records.
- **Excel report** — a formatted workbook with Summary, Membership, Vehicles, SoH History,
  and Service & Faults sheets. Where enough records exist, the summary includes charts for
  battery State of Health over time and service or fault events.

Both downloads are prepared for the signed-in member at the time they ask for them. Internal
account identifiers and security hashes are excluded. Only the final six characters of a VIN
are included because full VINs are not stored.

[Download a sample Excel report](/downloads/sample-ipace-owner-data.xlsx) to see the layout.
Every person, vehicle and event in the sample is fictional; it contains no member data.

## Keep your download safe

Your own export may contain your email address, vehicle registration, retained VIN reference,
battery readings, and notes about service or faults. The download is not cached by the
application, but the copy saved to your device is then under your control. Store it securely
and take care before sharing it.
