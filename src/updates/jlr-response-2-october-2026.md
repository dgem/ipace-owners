<p class="lead">We asked JLR for a fair, lasting answer to the high-voltage battery problems members have reported. Its 2 October response offers individual case review, but no commitment to a wider remedy. We had hoped for more.</p>

Full high-voltage battery replacement led our member survey. JLR has not committed to a
replacement programme or explained how continued case-by-case repairs will deliver a
durable result. We are disappointed, especially after members have described delays,
repeat workshop visits and uneven support. We will keep pressing for a clear plan.

## What JLR said

JLR says members' circumstances and model years differ, and some reported vehicles have
not been subject to battery campaign actions. It does not consider one remedy suitable
for everyone. It asks owners to provide their VIN and contact its Executive Office for
individual review. JLR says it wants to keep talking with the group; its engagement is
not an acceptance of liability or agreement with our concerns.

Each owner's circumstances matter. They do not explain why JLR cannot set out a
consistent approach to recurring battery and service problems. Our survey comments
describe delays, repeat visits and poor communication, alongside different faults.
These are self-reported experiences, not fleet-wide failure rates.

JLR's separate **H576 engineering programme** shows that a more targeted approach is
possible: its retailer instructions match selected VINs to specific battery modules for
free replacement, with a £500 voucher after the work. JLR describes this as a study of
a small number of 2019–2020 cars, not a finding that every selected module is faulty.

Members have also raised concerns about 2022-and-later cars. Campaign status can change. Owners continue to report first or repeat H570 notices.

On H570/H571, **JLR says** owners who have had the campaign carried out can drive, park
and charge as usual. Follow the advice for your own vehicle; we cannot assess its
condition from JLR's general statement.

## If you want JLR to review your case

Email **[UKEO@jaguarlandrover.com](mailto:UKEO@jaguarlandrover.com?subject=iPACE%20owners%20advocacy%20group)**
with **“iPACE owners advocacy group”** in the subject line. Include your VIN, as JLR has requested.
Briefly describe the fault, repairs, effect on you and outcome you seek, and keep
a copy. Contacting JLR is your choice. If many owners write, JLR will see the scale
its individual route must handle.

If you contact JLR, please [add it to your private member service history](/member/dashboard/).
Record when you emailed, when JLR acknowledged or replied, what it offered, whether the
outcome resolved your concern, and any diagnosis or repair costs you were asked to pay.
This gives us structured evidence of whether the Executive Office route delivers fair,
timely and lasting outcomes. A short summary is enough; keep correspondence and your VIN
private unless we ask for them securely.

We have not shared member records, VINs or survey responses with JLR. We would seek
your explicit permission before sharing your information ourselves.

We will review JLR's response with our advisers and update members on what next steps might look like.

## Correction to our meeting update

Our [25 September update](/updates/after-our-first-jlr-meeting/) said JLR had recognised
poor and inconsistent customer experiences. JLR clarified that it was *concerned to hear*
members describe those experiences and wanted to investigate them. We have corrected
the earlier wording.
