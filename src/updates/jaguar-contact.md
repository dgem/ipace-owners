Jaguar Land Rover has made initial contact with the I-PACE Owners' Advocacy Group. The response came within three working days of our approach, which is an encouraging start for an organisation of this size.

In an introductory telephone call, JLR acknowledged the concerns raised by owners, said it was taking them seriously, and welcomed the prospect of a constructive route to hear the voice of customers.

## What this means — and what it does not

This was a positive first conversation, but it was not yet a substantive discussion of solutions or commitments. We are therefore treating it as the beginning of a dialogue, rather than an outcome.

The JLR contact is now away for two weeks, and a further meeting appears likely after the August bank holiday. Our aim is to move from this initial contact to direct, meaningful discussions with the appropriate senior decision-makers by mid-September.

## Our next steps

We will prepare carefully for the next discussion, keep the engagement constructive, and make sure the concerns and experiences of owners are represented clearly. We will also keep members updated as matters develop.

For now, we are holding off on contacting the original recipients of our letter until we have a clearer understanding of how this JLR contact fits into the wider decision-making process.

## Help us return with a stronger voice

The period before the next discussion is an opportunity to strengthen both the group and the
evidence we can bring to the table. If you know another I-PACE owner, please encourage them to
[join the group](/join/): our shared target is 1,000 members.

If you are already a member, please also sign in to [My Data](/member/dashboard/) to register your
vehicle and add any available State of Health readings or service and fault records. Each complete
record helps us build a clearer, anonymised picture of the issues owners are experiencing.

The group remains independent and evidence-led. We welcome a serious conversation with JLR, while staying focused on securing fair and consistent outcomes for I-PACE owners.
