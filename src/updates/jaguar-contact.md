Jaguar Land Rover has made initial contact with the I-PACE Owners' Advocacy Group. The response came within three working days of our approach, which is an encouraging start for an organisation of this size.

Following that exploratory telephone call with Anna at JLR, Joel, one of the group's founding members, received a courteous thank-you and an invitation to meet the UK Client Care team. A date in the second week of September is being explored.

## What this means — and what it does not

This is a welcome step forward, but it is not yet a substantive discussion of solutions or commitments. The September date is tentative, and we are treating the invitation as the beginning of a dialogue rather than an outcome.

Our aim is to have direct, meaningful discussions with the appropriate people at JLR, informed by the real experiences of I-PACE owners.

## A short window to strengthen the evidence

Between now and the start of September, we have a practical opportunity to build the strongest possible anonymised evidence base. More members give the group a clearer voice; more vehicles, battery readings and service experiences make the picture we take into the meeting more useful and credible.

We will prepare carefully, keep the engagement constructive and independent, and make sure the concerns and experiences of owners are represented clearly. We will keep members updated as arrangements develop.

## Help us arrive prepared

If you know another I-PACE owner, please encourage them to [join the group](/join/). Our shared target is 1,000 members, and every member helps show the scale of the community we represent.

If you are already a member, please sign in to [My Data](/member/dashboard/) before September and add what you can:

- Your I-PACE vehicle
- Any available battery State of Health readings
- Your service, fault and repair history

Each record contributes to an anonymised picture of the issues owners are experiencing. That is the evidence we can responsibly reference when we meet JLR.

The group remains independent and evidence-led. We welcome this constructive engagement with JLR while staying focused on fair and consistent outcomes for I-PACE owners.
