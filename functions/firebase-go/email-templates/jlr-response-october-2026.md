## We hoped for more

JLR's 2 October response offers individual case review, but no commitment to a lasting
solution for the high-voltage battery problems members have reported. Full battery
replacement led our survey. JLR has not committed to a programme or explained how
continuing case-by-case repairs will resolve the wider problem. We are disappointed.

## Your case

[UKEO@jaguarlandrover.com](mailto:UKEO@jaguarlandrover.com?subject=iPACE%20owners%20advocacy%20group)
with **“iPACE owners advocacy group”** in the subject line. Include your VIN, a brief
history, the effect on you and what you want JLR to do. Keep a copy. Contacting JLR
is your choice.

and would ask your permission before doing so.

You can also ask JLR to address the wider problem and engage with us as a group.

If you contact JLR, please [tell us](mailto:contact@ipace-owners.org?subject=JLR%20Executive%20Office%20outcome)

diagnosis or repair. A short summary is enough; please keep your VIN and correspondence
private unless we request them securely.

We will review the response with our advisers and update you on next steps.
