## What is under way

- Tim is helping lead UK legal preparation with new volunteers, with local leads joining as other regions come on line. Please record your legal name and formal contact address privately in My Data. This does not commit you to legal action, but is a prerequisite for any future legal process.
- Ole in Norway and Kevin in France are leading our Northern and Southern Europe efforts. Kevin is a long-time contributor to [I-PACE Forums](https://www.ipaceforums.co.uk/f). Thank you to them and to everyone volunteering.
- Member stories, with photos or video and separate publication permission, are in final testing. Register your interest in trying this and other new features in My Data.
- Secure document imports are being developed to make evidence collection easier.

JLR's case-by-case response still leaves an important question unanswered: if a module fails outside the high-voltage warranty, is it a safety concern or chargeable degradation? Diagnosis and replacement can cost hundreds or thousands of pounds. H572 is still unannounced, so its scope and implications for future module failures remain unknown.

Please check your vehicles are registered, record service outcomes and costs, and add your Executive Office response experience if you contacted JLR directly. Help us reach more owners and volunteer your skills if you can. Stronger together.
