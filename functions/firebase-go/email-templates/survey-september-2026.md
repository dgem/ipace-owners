Hi {{memberFirstName}},

Our founding member will meet Jaguar Land Rover **the day after this survey closes**. Please take a few minutes to tell us which outcomes you would support us putting to JLR.

The **Preferred outcomes — September meeting** survey is open from **4 to 23 September 2026**. You can choose more than one outcome.

[Have your say — tell us what you need](https://ipace-owners.org/member/survey-response/?id=survey_38447815d17b0e954a4edbca1b9600c9){.button}

The choices are:

- **Full HV Replacement** — replace all affected HV modules and associated components, with fair mobility support or inconvenience payments where a vehicle is range-limited or awaiting parts.
- **A Fair Buy Back** — a transparent, fair price based on age, usage and/or the cost of a full HV replacement, rather than the current market value.
- **Fair Compensation** — recognise the disruption caused by recalls, quality issues, long waits for parts and repeated workshop visits.
- **Additional Concerns** — raise another issue that Jaguar Land Rover needs to address.
- **None of the above** — if these choices do not meet your needs.
