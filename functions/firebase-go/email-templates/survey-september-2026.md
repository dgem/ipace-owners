Hi {{memberFirstName}},

Our founding member will meet Jaguar Land Rover **the day after this survey closes**. Please take a few minutes to tell us which outcomes you would support us putting to JLR.

The **Preferred outcomes — September meeting** survey is open from **4 to 23 September 2026**. You can choose more than one outcome.

[Take the survey](https://ipace-owners.org/member/survey-response/?id=survey_a2371fc4a6efb138c8ac117b156d5d3f)

The choices are:

- **Full HV Replacement** — replace all affected HV modules and associated components, with fair mobility support or inconvenience payments where a vehicle is range-limited or awaiting parts.
- **A Fair Buy Back** — a transparent, fair price based on age, usage and/or the cost of a full HV replacement, rather than the current market value.
- **Fair Compensation** — recognise the disruption caused by recalls, quality issues, long waits for parts and repeated workshop visits.
- **Additional Concerns** — raise another issue that Jaguar Land Rover needs to address.
- **None of the above** — if these choices do not meet your needs.

## Help make the evidence harder to ignore

We now have **1,299 members** (about 1.3K), **568 cars registered**, **96 State of Health readings** and **135 service and fault records**. That is a strong start, but the evidence becomes much more persuasive as more owners add their experience.

Our next milestones are **1,500 members** and **1,000+ cars registered**, with more battery-health and service records. At **3,000 members**, we would represent roughly 10% of I-PACE owners. Reaching 3,000 members with 3,000 cars recorded would give JLR data that is very difficult to dismiss.

Please add what you can to [My Data](https://ipace-owners.org/member/dashboard/): your car, any State of Health readings, and your recall, service or fault experience. Each contribution strengthens the anonymised evidence we can take to JLR.

Thank you for helping us make the meeting count.
